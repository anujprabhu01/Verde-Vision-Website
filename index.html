why it wins

Five familiar problems.

what it does

Everything you need to
design with confidence.

Easy to use, with an intuitive user interface. The plants you're working with sit in a seven-slot inventory bar in front of you - pick one, look where it goes, pinch, it's planted.

  1. 01

    Design in real space

    Drop life-size plants into the real yard and walk around them at true mature scale - before you buy a thing.

  2. 02

    Estimate on the spot

    Every plant you place is tallied from the prices you set on the dashboard. Add labor with one toggle, or leave it off. Export an itemized PDF while you're still standing in the yard.

  3. 03

    Draw the blueprint as you go

    Mark the house, the boundary and the features as you walk the property. The scaled PDF builds itself from what you mapped and everything you placed, including plants, lights and boulders.

  4. 04

    Night mode and mature view

    Place LED fixtures, then flip the yard to night and watch them shine across the plants. Switch to mature view and every plant grows to full size, so spacing and future scale are settled before anything goes in the ground.

  5. 05

    Hand your client the headset

    Give them the headset and tell them to give two thumbs up. They watch their yard grow to maturity right before their eyes. Tell them one thumbs down, and they see it lit up at night. No setup, no eye calibration, nothing to learn.

  6. 06

    Pick up where you left off

    Three printed plates go up around the property - on a wall, a post, a fence, anything that won't move - and stay there. Weeks later, look at them through the headset and the whole design snaps back exactly where you left it - for you, or for the crew.

night mode

Walk your yard during the day, see it lit up at night.

Lighting is one of the hardest things for people to visualize. A plan can show where the fixtures go, but not what the yard will look like at night. Now it's possible. Your client stands in their own yard and watches the lights come on.

And the best part: the client does it themselves. A thumbs-down takes the yard to night, a thumbs-up brings the daylight back.

Thumbs-up and thumbs-down: the same two gestures in the headset.

the catalog

A library of the Desert Southwest that keeps growing.

We scan real plants whenever we can so that there's no confusion for the client about what it will look like planted. The ones too big or too leafy to scan - most trees - we model in 3D.
